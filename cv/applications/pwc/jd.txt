Technology and AI Graduate Programmes – London

With a human-led, tech-powered approach, we help organisations to take advantage of the latest technologies and deliver change, at pace.

So our people build technical knowledge and commercial understanding on how technology is revolutionising the way business works.

Apply for these programmes if:
- You love solving complex problems
- You are always looking for ways to improve things
- You want to work at the cutting edge of tech
- You have an agile mind and like constantly learning

Joining a Graduate programme

We offer you the chance to explore opportunities with us based on your interests, location and degree. We provide clear guidance throughout our process to support you in making the decision that's right for you. It's an exciting time to join us. We're a skills-enabled organisation, allowing us to identify, develop and future-proof the skills our people need today and for tomorrow. By focusing on human and technical skills, we're promoting cross-functional teamwork, driving inclusive growth, and maximising value for our clients. And by doing this, we do more than develop skills – we cultivate potential. Enabling you to develop the skills you need, for the career you want. So that you can grow here and go further.

In London we have opportunities in the business areas listed below. If you have a particular preference on business area, you'll have the opportunity to express this during the application process. Please note, preferences are considered but not guaranteed.

- Cybersecurity - No PQ
- Engineering, Data Science and Analytics - CFAB (Certificate in Finance, Accounting and Business)
- Engineering, Data Science and Analytics - No PQ
- Financial Crime & Forensics - No PQ

What you'll need

You'll need to have achieved or be on track to pass an undergraduate degree or above in any subject. We determine whether you're on course to pass based on the grades you achieved in your last completed year of academic study.

Please note that for certain roles in Cybersecurity and Financial Crime and Forensics you may need to have studied a STEM degree subject.

As a reminder we accept one application per recruitment season. Before submitting your application, please ensure you have taken the time to research your chosen business category and business area thoroughly, and have a good appreciation of the professional qualifications aligned to your preferences.
