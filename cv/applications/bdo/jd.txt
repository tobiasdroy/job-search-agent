What does the TRA controls team do?

The Technology Risk Assurance Controls team helps the audit team identify and assess risks associated with the client's finance applications. We focus on IT systems and processes related to access, change management and automated interfaces between systems. When we identify weaknesses in the client IT environment, we provide recommendations to management to help them strengthen processes and controls. We also work closely with the financial audit team to explain the risks arising from IT that impact our audit approach and may require additional audit testing of financial statement integrity.

Key responsibilities include:
-IT General Controls testing over the financial applications
-Cyber Security Risk Assessments
-Database and Operating Systems controls testing
-Assessment of automated controls operated by the business

What does the TRA Data team do?

The TRA Data team uses data analytics to support audits by examining entire populations of financial data, rather than relying solely on traditional sample-based testing. By combining technology, data analysis and audit expertise, we help provide efficient, high-quality assurance across a range of financial statement areas.

Key responsibilities include:
-Exploring and analysing data to help understand how businesses operate and identify potential risks using tools such as Alteryx, Databricks, Python, SQL, SAS, amongst others
-Checking and validating financial information to support the audit process
-Working closely with colleagues across audit teams to solve problems and deliver successful outcomes
-Presenting insights in a clear and engaging way to help teams focus on key areas of risk, including building visualisations and dashboards
